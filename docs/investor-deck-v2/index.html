The Core Friction

Collections live in drawers, spreadsheets and shoeboxes

Collecting remains fundamentally fragmented across manual spreadsheets, outdated offline appraisal methods, impersonal mass marketplaces, and pervasive under-insurance.

01 • Fragmentation
Spreadsheets & Memory

Items sit untracked in albums, tins, and Excel files. No single system exists to photograph, catalog, track valuations, and share pieces seamlessly.

Zero automated record-keeping
02 • Valuation Friction
Slow, Opaque & Costly

Traditional appraisal means in-person visits, postage risk and a fee for every item. Most collectors remain completely blind to current market value.

Weeks of delay for basic estimates
03 • Unfriendly Commerce
Generic Platforms Fail

eBay and Facebook Marketplace treat rare Victorian stamps exactly like used toaster ovens: no category grading, no provenance, rampant counterfeits, and fees built for general goods.

High friction & low community trust
04 • Protection Void
Total Insurance Blindspot

Underwriters require itemised appraisals, receipts, and professional certificates before writing cover. Because documenting is onerous, collectors leave collections uninsured.

Massive unaddressed risk
The Reality Today
Valuable assets locked in physical opacity
No live valuations. No insurance inventory. No audience.
