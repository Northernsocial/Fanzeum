The Core Friction

Collections live in drawers, spreadsheets and shoeboxes

No single place to identify, value, show, trade and protect; valuation is slow and expensive; general marketplaces (eBay) are not built for collectors; insurance is ignored.

01 • Fragmentation
Spreadsheets & Memory

Items sit untracked in albums, tins, and Excel files. No single system exists to photograph, catalog, track valuations, and share pieces seamlessly.

Zero automated record-keeping
02 • Valuation Friction
Slow, Opaque & Costly

Traditional expert appraisal requires in-person salon visits, postage risk, and £50–£200 fees per item. Most collectors remain completely blind to current market value.

Weeks of delay for basic estimates
03 • Unfriendly Commerce
Generic Platforms Fail

eBay and Facebook Marketplace treat rare Victorian stamps exactly like used toaster ovens: no category grading, no provenance, rampant counterfeits, and 13%+ fees.

High friction & low community trust
04 • Protection Void
Total Insurance Blindspot

Underwriters require itemised appraisals, receipts, and professional certificates before writing cover. Because documenting is onerous, collectors leave collections uninsured.

Massive unaddressed risk
